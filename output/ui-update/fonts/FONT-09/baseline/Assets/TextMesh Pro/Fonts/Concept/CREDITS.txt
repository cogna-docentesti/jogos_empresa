Neither acquired notice declares a Reserved Font Name.

Static files are generated derivatives: FontTools 4.60.1 instances of the
official upright variable fonts. Roboto: wght=400/700, wdth=100 (normal width).
Roboto Condensed: its own family, wght=400/500/600/700. All variable axes are
fully pinned; no simulated bold, italic, or geometric width scaling is applied.
Family/style naming records are normalized for explicit Unity/FreeType faces;
glyph outlines and horizontal metrics match serialized official instances.

Revisions, final acquisition URLs, hashes, original names, applied axes, and
coverage gaps: docs/ui-update/font-sources.json.
Reproduction: tools/ui/font02_sources.py and tools/ui/requirements-font02.txt.

These are TTF sources. TMP atlases, materials, fallback policy, and scene
integration are later tasks. Do not point production references at research
downloads, Examples & Extras, or fonts installed on the operating system.
